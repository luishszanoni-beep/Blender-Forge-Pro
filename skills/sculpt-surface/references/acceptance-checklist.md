# Sculpt & Surface Specialist | Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] Every requested relief feature has a stable feature ID.
- [ ] Actual mesh/evaluated geometry exists; bump disabled in proof view.
- [ ] Wireframe plus clay closeup show relief and shape.
- [ ] Where feasible, profile/depth or before/after mesh comparison recorded.
- [ ] Relief matches direction/scale and reference placement.
- [ ] No unwanted change to approved outer silhouette.
- [ ] Exported derivative preserves mandatory geometric relief.
- [ ] Unknown or unverified proof remains PENDING, never PASS.

When any required item is unchecked, record a blocker or explicit `PENDING`/`UNKNOWN`; do not invent a passing test.
