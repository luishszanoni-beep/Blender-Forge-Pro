# Material Specialist | Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] Materials mapped to stable part IDs with reference IDs.
- [ ] Roughness/metallic look plausible under neutral light.
- [ ] Bump-only details not claimed as geometric.
- [ ] UV scale and orientation inspected for repeated patterns.
- [ ] Exported PBR behavior is explicitly checked for GLB.
- [ ] Missing textures are recorded as blocked.
- [ ] Relevant materials are reusable across permitted product variants.
- [ ] Source materials retained editable and labeled.

When any required item is unchecked, record a blocker or explicit `PENDING`/`UNKNOWN`; do not invent a passing test.
